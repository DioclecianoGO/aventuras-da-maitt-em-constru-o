# Lyra — Identity Reference

Use this folder for real photographs or approved visual references of **Lyra**.

## Identity purpose

Lyra is a **tricolor mixed-breed cat**. These references exist to preserve her recognizable real-world identity when translated into the stylized visual language of Aventuras da Maittê.

Canonical personality direction for acting/design:

- observant;
- calm;
- attentive to details;
- precise / quietly analytical.

## Preserve from the references

Prioritize:

- real tricolor coat distribution;
- face markings;
- face shape and muzzle;
- ear shape and placement;
- eye shape/color when visible;
- body proportions;
- tail shape/carriage;
- recognizable posture/silhouette;
- distinctive markings/features visible in the approved photos.

## Do not

- simplify her into a generic calico/tricolor cat if that loses her real markings;
- give her Will's mischievous/exploratory acting personality;
- permanently bind her to a subject or Skill;
- make her photorealistic if it conflicts with the project character language.

## Acting direction

Useful future production poses may include:

- calm attentive idle;
- focused observation;
- looking closely at a detail;
- quiet hint/reaction;
- satisfied success reaction;
- graceful movement.

These are acting directions, not permanent gameplay assignments.
