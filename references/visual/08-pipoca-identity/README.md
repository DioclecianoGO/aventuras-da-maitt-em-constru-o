# Pipoca — Identity Reference

Use this folder for real photographs or approved visual references of **Pipoca**.

## Identity purpose

Pipoca is a **white Maltese with a slightly curly coat**. These references exist to preserve his recognizable real-world identity when he is translated into the stylized visual language of Aventuras da Maittê.

Canonical personality direction for acting/design:

- adventurous;
- energetic;
- brave;
- eager to move/explore.

## Preserve from the references

Prioritize:

- Maltese anatomy and proportions;
- white coat and real coat texture/volume;
- face shape, muzzle and ear placement;
- eye/nose relationship;
- tail carriage;
- recognizable posture and silhouette;
- any distinctive real markings/features visible in the approved photos.

## Do not

- turn Pipoca into a generic white puppy;
- exaggerate him into a baby/chibi mascot;
- give him traits that belong to Will, Lyra or Burpee;
- permanently bind him to a school subject or Skill;
- make the result photorealistic if it conflicts with the project character language.

## Acting direction

When a production character sheet is eventually created, useful poses may include:

- eager/ready-to-go idle;
- adventurous entrance;
- excited discovery;
- brave encouragement;
- success reaction;
- energetic movement.

These are acting directions, not permanent gameplay assignments.
