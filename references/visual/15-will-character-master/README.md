# Will Character Master

Status: PROVISIONAL — CHARACTER MASTER CANDIDATE PENDING EXPLICIT APPROVAL

## Purpose

This folder holds the approved identity direction and supporting material for Will, the orange mixed-breed cat companion in Aventuras da Maittê.

The Character Master is the identity authority for Will. It is not automatically a runtime sprite and must not be referenced directly by game/domain code.

## Canonical approved filename

After explicit visual approval, save the approved master as:

`WILL_MASTER_APPROVED.png`

## Identity direction

Will must remain recognizably the same individual represented by the approved master and real-life reference photos.

Key identity traits to preserve:

- orange tabby domestic cat;
- white chest/bib and white paws;
- green to yellow-green eyes;
- pink nose;
- large upright triangular ears;
- clear tabby markings on forehead, face, body and tail;
- long expressive whiskers;
- medium-to-large adult-cat proportions;
- long orange tabby tail;
- curious, investigative, playful expression;
- clearly feline anatomy and silhouette.

## Style direction

- original 2D storybook / coloring-book illustration;
- warm watercolor texture and confident linework;
- simplified and expressive rather than photorealistic;
- compatible with the Maittê, Burpee and Pipoca Character Master family;
- readable at tablet scale;
- no direct imitation of any named artist.
