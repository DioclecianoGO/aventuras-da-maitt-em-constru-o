# Maittê — Protagonist

**Status:** DECIDED concept; final character sheet PROVISIONAL.

Maittê is represented by the protagonist in the MVP. Future versions may allow other avatars, clothing and customization.

## Visual identity
The character is inspired by the supplied real-world references but must not be realistic. Direction: 2D line-art/storybook, coloring-book warmth, with a light manga/anime influence. Preserve approximately eight-year-old proportions; avoid baby/chibi, adult-looking or photorealistic treatment.

Approved traits:
- dark brown hair slightly below the shoulders;
- light waves/curls at the tips;
- marked fringe/bangs;
- subtle bleached/light lock designed as an independently controllable layer so it may later be recolored or removed;
- pink glasses as the current canonical MVP direction, but architecturally compatible with a future accessory slot;
- skirt as a recurring clothing preference;
- high-top canvas sneakers without protected branding;
- colorful socks;
- shirt with a green heart representing hope.

## Color arc
The villain steals most of Maittê's colors. **The green heart remains saturated at opening and is the primary visual hope anchor.**

Other character regions may recover color at progress milestones. The character asset should support independently controllable visual regions such as glasses, hair streak, shirt areas, skirt, socks and shoes.

**DECIDED:** the exact restoration order of those regions is not fixed in Phase 1A. Demonstrating stolen + one or more partial states must remain configurable and must not accidentally establish a permanent reward order.

## Required scales
The design should remain recognizable at:
- hero/cutscene scale;
- gameplay/challenge scale;
- small World Board avatar scale;
- future profile/icon scale.

## Expressions and feedback
Supported direction includes joy, curiosity, concentration, surprise, doubt, celebration, determination and mild frustration/thoughtfulness. Mistakes should use curious/thinking feedback rather than punitive sadness.

## Animation ambition
Maittê should feel alive through idle movement, expressive reactions and movement between board spaces. Rich animation is not an MVP blocker; simpler translate/hop/fade treatments are valid fallbacks while preserving the game loop.
