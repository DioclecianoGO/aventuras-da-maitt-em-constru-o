# Maittê — Protagonist

**Status:** DECIDED concept/acting requirements; final character sheet PROVISIONAL.

Maittê is represented by the protagonist in the MVP. Future versions may allow other avatars, clothing and customization.

## Visual identity

The character is inspired by the supplied real-world references but must not be realistic. Direction: 2D line-art/storybook, coloring-book warmth, with a light manga/anime influence.

Preserve approximately eight-year-old proportions; avoid baby/chibi, adult-looking or photorealistic treatment.

Approved traits:

- dark brown hair slightly below the shoulders;
- light waves/curls at the tips;
- marked fringe/bangs;
- subtle bleached/light lock designed as an independently controllable layer so it may later be recolored or removed;
- pink glasses as the current canonical MVP direction, but architecturally compatible with a future accessory slot;
- skirt as a recurring clothing preference;
- high-top canvas sneakers without protected branding;
- colorful socks;
- shirt with a green heart representing hope.

## Color arc

The villain steals most of Maittê's colors. **The green heart remains saturated at opening and is the primary visual hope anchor.**

Other character regions may recover color at progress milestones. The character asset should support independently controllable visual regions such as:

- glasses;
- hair streak;
- hair;
- shirt;
- skirt;
- socks;
- shoes.

**DECIDED:** the exact restoration order of those regions is not fixed in Phase 1A/1B. Demonstrating stolen + one or more partial states must remain configurable and must not accidentally establish a permanent reward order.

## Required scales

The design should remain recognizable at:

- hero/cutscene scale;
- gameplay/challenge scale;
- small World Board avatar scale;
- future profile/icon scale.

## Acting system

Maittê must feel like a participant in the learning interaction, not a marker pasted onto the Board.

Minimum Phase 1B acting states:

- **idle-curious** — alive/alert while waiting;
- **listen-think** — visibly attends to companion/task;
- **success** — joy/pride and attention toward the restored result;
- **retry-thinking** — mild puzzlement/consideration, never shame/sadness;
- **move** — simple Board travel treatment.

Recommended reusable expressions/poses include joy, curiosity, concentration, surprise, doubt, celebration, determination, pointing/looking and greeting.

Character acting should help direct attention. For example, when a pet speaks, Maittê can look/listen; after restoration she can look toward the newly colored area.

## Feedback tone

Mistakes should use curious/thinking feedback rather than punitive sadness.

Do not use crying, defeat or disappointment as the default response to an incorrect educational attempt.

## Animation ambition

Maittê should feel alive through idle movement, expressive reactions and movement between Board spaces.

Phase 1B requires a lightweight acting foundation but not a full skeletal animation system. Valid techniques include layered SVG groups, frame swaps, CSS transforms, small hops/walk cycles and fades.

Richer animation may replace these assets later without changing game-domain/evaluation logic.
