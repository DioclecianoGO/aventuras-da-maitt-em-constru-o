# Main Villain

**Status:** DECIDED concept; final illustration PROVISIONAL.

## Character logic
He did not begin evil. He stopped learning, lost curiosity, became gray and was overtaken by a spell that made him want to remove beauty/color from everyone else's world. His arc must preserve the idea that continued learning and curiosity restore perspective.

## Approved visual characteristics
- can fly;
- stocky/fat silhouette;
- purple skin/body direction;
- bald crown with green hair around the sides/back, with a comedic old-magician silhouette;
- orange moustache;
- large round/goggle-like glasses;
- magician's robe/mantle;
- magical wand shaped as a pencil;
- the pencil-wand absorbs/sucks colors from objects and environments.

These are design ingredients, not instructions to copy an existing copyrighted character. Final design must be original.

## Behavior
He steals colors and creative/coloring tools. His minions can carry individual stolen tools. His tone may be theatrical/comedic enough for a child-facing adventure; he must not become visually frightening or cruel.

## Ending
When Maittê restores the colors, the spell is broken. His consequence is to return to learning/school, not violent punishment.
