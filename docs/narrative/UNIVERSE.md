# Universe — Aventuras da Maittê

**Status:** DECIDED except where marked.

## Premise
A magical villain lost curiosity and gradually became gray after he stopped learning. Under a spell, he concluded that if he could no longer see beauty in the world, nobody should. He steals the world's colors and also the tools used to create color: pencils, crayons, markers, paints, watercolors and related objects.

Maittê retains a green heart on her shirt: **hope**. Hope is the surviving color and narrative reason she sets out to recover the others. Learning restores color to Maittê and to the world.

## Resolution
The story is restorative, not destructive. Maittê eventually breaks the spell through recovered learning/colors. The villain is not annihilated; he returns to school to rediscover learning and curiosity.

## Minions
Each major minion may hold/use a stolen coloring tool. Minions should preferentially be animals or creatures coherent with the habitat/biome in which they appear. After restoration they may reveal a good nature, remain in that world and reappear later as friendly recurring characters.

## Worlds
The universe has a shared origin/overworld and six subject-worlds: Mathematics, Portuguese, Science, Geography, History and English. Subject worlds are independently selectable from the origin. Future updates may add regions without redesigning the core navigation.

Worlds are narrative/game containers for curriculum; curriculum must never be distorted to justify scenery.

## Companions
Maittê's pets are recurring inhabitants and contextual guides:
- Burpee — blue-merle Border Collie, blue eyes; intelligent/reasoning-oriented.
- Pipoca — white Maltese, slightly curly fur; adventurous and energetic.
- Will — orange mixed-breed cat; curious.
- Lyra — tricolor mixed-breed cat; observant.

They may naturally encounter Maittê in the environment and introduce a challenge. They support comprehension but do not solve the task.
