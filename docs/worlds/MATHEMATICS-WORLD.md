# Mathematics World

**Status:** DECIDED structure; biome/art details PROVISIONAL.

## Purpose
The Mathematics World is the first playable subject-world in the MVP. It must be capable of receiving multiple Mathematics content slices without binding scenery permanently to a specific skill.

## Provisional identity
Working name: **Deserto dos Números / Number Desert**.

The desert direction is useful for Phase 1A visual planning, but remains PROVISIONAL until concept art is approved.

## Structural rules
1. The Mathematics World is a subject container, not a curriculum container.
2. The same world must host Number Sense, Addition/Subtraction, Problems and Plane Geometry through different Zones/routes/Activity Slots without redesigning the world.
3. Do not create permanent scenery whose meaning hardcodes a curricular topic.
4. Local progression may be sequential while subject choice remains open globally.
5. The world begins largely line-art/desaturated and regains color progressively.
6. Completion progress also appears on the Mathematics region of the Overworld.

## MVP delivery slices
- **Slice A — Number Sense:** first end-to-end technical/content slice.
- **Slice B — Addition, Subtraction and Problems:** second useful study path.
- **Slice C — Plane Geometry:** third useful study path.

These content slices do not dictate permanent scenery names.

## Phase 1A first-board visual target
The first visual Board should represent the opening Zone rather than starting at a later oasis region.

**PROVISIONAL preferred opening Zone:** **Dunas Douradas**.

Direction for concept review:
- broad golden-dune/line-art landscape with a curved route embedded in scenery;
- Maittê starts at the near/entry side of the Zone;
- Activity Slots are scenery objects along the route rather than buttons;
- at least one orientation landmark is visible;
- a more distant destination may be visible for anticipation;
- **Oásis dos Enigmas** may appear as the next-Zone preview/direction, rather than being the starting point of this Board.

The names `Dunas Douradas` and `Oásis dos Enigmas` remain PROVISIONAL visual/narrative labels until art review. They do not own Number Sense or any other skill.

A distant pyramid/ruin silhouette can be explored as an atmosphere/anticipation concept, but exact landmark choice is not DECIDED and must not be named after a mathematical operation/skill.

## Visual configuration boundary
Presentation metadata such as scenery-object choice, slot artwork or asset identifiers belongs in a separate world/scenery visual configuration keyed by stable slot/zone ids. Do not add art URLs or presentation-specific `visualKind` values to the educational/domain schema merely for Phase 1A.

Existing neutral spatial anchors may be consumed by the visual layer.

## Landmarks and minions
**PROVISIONAL / GAP:** exact landmarks and the Mathematics minion are unresolved.

Constraints:
- child-friendly illustrated storybook direction;
- minion can be an animal/creature coherent with the habitat;
- it may carry a stolen coloring tool;
- restoration can turn it into a friendly recurring resident;
- landmarks create orientation/anticipation and do not own curriculum.

## Companion use
Burpee, Pipoca, Will or Lyra may appear organically and introduce challenges. Companion assignment by skill remains configurable and must not be hardcoded.

## Transition
Overworld → Mathematics uses spatial zoom. World Board → Challenge Stage preserves spatial/perceptual continuity; exact nested/overlay routing remains an implementation technique, not the product requirement itself.
