# Mathematics World

**Status:** DECIDED structure; biome/art details PROVISIONAL.

## Purpose

The Mathematics World is the first playable subject-world in the MVP. It must be capable of receiving multiple Mathematics content slices without binding scenery permanently to a specific skill.

## Provisional identity

Working name: **Deserto dos Números / Number Desert**.

This name and desert direction are useful for planning the first visual slice, but remain PROVISIONAL until final world art is approved.

## Structural rules

1. The Mathematics World is a subject container, not a curriculum container.
2. The same world must be able to host Number Sense, Addition/Subtraction, Problems and Plane Geometry through different regions, routes, landmarks or Activity Slots without redesigning the entire world.
3. Do not create permanent scenery whose meaning hardcodes a curricular topic in a way that prevents later reassignment.
4. World progression remains sequential locally where appropriate, while subject selection remains open globally from the Overworld.
5. The world begins largely line-art/desaturated and regains color progressively as Activity Slots are completed.
6. Completion progress must also be reflected in the Mathematics region on the Overworld.

## MVP region model

The approved plan must accommodate three internal delivery slices:

- **Slice A — Number Sense:** first end-to-end vertical slice.
- **Slice B — Addition, Subtraction and Problems:** second useful study path.
- **Slice C — Plane Geometry:** third useful study path.

The exact landmarks, route length, visual subregions and minions for these slices are not yet DECIDED. Lovable may propose options in Plan Mode, but proposals are not product decisions until reviewed.

## Landmarks and minions

**PROVISIONAL / GAP:** specific landmarks and the Mathematics World minion are intentionally unresolved.

Constraints for proposals:
- preserve the child-friendly illustrated storybook direction;
- minions may be animals/creatures coherent with the habitat;
- each major minion may carry a stolen coloring tool;
- restored minions may remain in the world as friendly recurring characters;
- landmarks create anticipation and orientation, but must not own curricular content.

## Companion use

Burpee, Pipoca, Will or Lyra may appear organically in the Mathematics World and introduce challenges. Companion assignment by skill remains to be specified; the implementation plan must not hardcode a permanent pedagogical role without approval.

## Transition

Overworld → Mathematics World uses the approved spatial zoom model. World Board → Challenge Stage preserves spatial continuity and must not feel like leaving the game for a conventional form page.
