# Overworld

**Status:** DECIDED structure and reviewed production-composition locks; remaining exact scenic geometry/art/names PROVISIONAL.

## Spatial model
The home experience is a large illustrated map containing a shared origin/base and six subject-world destinations. The player may choose any subject from the origin; Mathematics does not globally unlock Portuguese, etc.

The map reads as one coherent magical geography rather than six dashboard cards. Each world is visually identifiable as a biome/region and can show its color-restoration progress directly in the illustration.

## Base da Esperança
**DECIDED:** Base da Esperança is the central narrative/home origin, not a seventh subject world.

It should sit approximately central in the world composition so the six subject destinations feel independently reachable rather than sequentially ordered around one “first” subject.

Possible environmental ingredients such as Maittê's house, a small plaza/tree or the pets remain concept-level details. Do not silently redefine the Base as a camping outpost or introduce a new magical artifact system merely to decorate it.

## Preferred provisional geography
The following arrangement preserves the geography developed during product definition and is the preferred starting composition for Phase 1A, but remains visually reviewable:
- northwest/west: Portuguese / Floresta das Letras;
- north: History / Reino do Tempo, with mountain/height separation;
- east/northeast: Geography / Vale dos Exploradores;
- east/southeast: English city region;
- southwest: Mathematics / Deserto dos Números;
- south/coast: Science / Oceano das Descobertas;
- Base da Esperança: approximately central.

Transitions between biomes should be organic rather than rectangular tiles: forest→fields/valley, valley→roads/city, dunes→coast, mountains→kingdom, etc.

Exact positions may be adjusted during visual concept review when composition quality requires it; such changes are PROVISIONAL until approved.

## Reviewed production-composition locks

The complementary visual north stars approved under `references/visual/11-visual-production-slice-01-output/` add these **DECIDED** production constraints:

- the Overworld is framed as one landscape adventure/coloring-book notebook page;
- spiral binding sits on the **LEFT EDGE**, not at the center of a two-page spread;
- subject regions form one continuous geography and visually flow into neighboring regions rather than becoming separated cards/islands;
- the illustration should contain a high density of small closed/colorable scenic details;
- Base da Esperança remains approximately central;
- Maittê and the four pets belong physically at/near the Base in the reviewed home-world direction, subject to staging/readability refinement;
- the approved reference images guide character quality and connected-map composition complementarily; neither is a permanent flattened runtime background.

The exact final drawing, landmark positions and scenic micro-layout remain reviewable. These presentation refinements must not imply a global subject order or change route/progression semantics.

## Opening color state
The Overworld begins near-complete line art / stolen-color treatment.

**DECIDED:** Maittê's green heart is the primary saturated color at opening. Do not add an unrelated fully saturated green flag, lantern or other Base prop that competes with the heart as the narrative hope anchor unless later approved.

Do not simulate progression using arbitrary pre-painted/radial region tinting. Restored color must remain derived from gameplay progress through replaceable presentation/restoration layers or units.

## Entry interaction
Selecting a world triggers a gradual spatial zoom toward that region, creating the sensation of physically entering the world. Major narrative/context transitions may complement this with the page-turn metaphor, but page-turn does not replace spatial zoom.

## Progress visualization
As progress occurs inside a world:
1. sections of that world's Board regain color;
2. the corresponding Overworld region regains color;
3. progress should be understandable visually before reading a percentage.

## Navigation
Do not use a conventional persistent application header as the primary child-facing navigation. The folded world map is the diegetic return-to-Overworld affordance inside subject worlds; Maittê's backpack is a distinct secondary/future concept governed by `docs/ux/NAVIGATION.md`.

## Production composition

Production Overworld art follows `docs/design/ASSET-PRODUCTION-PIPELINE.md` and `docs/design/VISUAL-ASSET-CONTRACT.md`.

**DECIDED:** approved north-star imagery is art direction, not permission to flatten the Overworld into one permanent image or redraw it as one giant code-generated SVG. Runtime composition must remain sufficiently separable for characters, restoration, foreground, interaction hit areas and later asset replacement.

## Expansion
The geography must allow future regions/updates without redesigning the core model.
