# Overworld

**Status:** DECIDED structure; world art/names PROVISIONAL.

## Spatial model
The home experience is a large illustrated map containing a shared origin/base and six subject-world destinations. The player may choose any subject from the origin; Mathematics does not globally unlock Portuguese, etc.

The map should read as one coherent magical geography rather than six dashboard cards. Each world is visually identifiable as a biome/region and can show its color-restoration progress directly in the illustration.

## Entry interaction
Selecting a world triggers a gradual spatial zoom toward that region, creating the sensation of physically entering the world. Major narrative/context transitions may complement this with the approved page-turn metaphor, but page-turn must not replace spatial zoom everywhere.

## Progress visualization
Initially affected regions are largely line-art/desaturated. As progress occurs inside a world:
1. sections of that world's board regain color;
2. the corresponding region/thumbnail/area on the Overworld also regains color;
3. progress should therefore be understandable visually before reading a percentage.

## Navigation
Do not use a conventional persistent application header as the primary child-facing navigation. Navigation should be diegetic/subtle: e.g. map/book/backpack-like affordances. Detailed profile/settings can live in secondary screens.

## Expansion
The geography must allow future regions/updates without redesigning the core model.
