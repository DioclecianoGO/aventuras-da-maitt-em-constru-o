# Challenge Stage

**Status:** DECIDED lifecycle.

Challenge Stage is the focused interaction state entered from a board Activity Slot.

## Lifecycle
1. Current board space is selected/activated.
2. A companion or contextual scene may explain the mission using short text + spoken instruction/visual demonstration where appropriate.
3. Puzzle Template renders the assigned Content Pack/activity.
4. Player acts through touch-first interaction.
5. Immediate formative feedback is shown.
6. Hints/support may escalate according to Support Level.
7. Success closes/resolves the challenge.
8. Return to the same world board context.
9. Color restoration and reward/progress feedback occur.
10. Maittê advances to the next board space.

## Child UX
- touch targets must be tablet-friendly;
- instructions must be short and may be replayed;
- do not depend exclusively on reading;
- avoid modal clutter and conventional form aesthetics;
- retry should feel natural, not punitive;
- companion guidance must not reveal the answer unless the activity is explicitly in Discover mode.

## Audio/microphone
Spoken instruction/audio support is part of the product direction. Microphone-based spoken answers are a future-capable Challenge Template and are not required for the first Mathematics slice unless separately approved.
