# Navigation

**Status:** DECIDED foundation; placement/art details PROVISIONAL.

## Principle
The primary child-facing navigation must feel like part of the adventure. A conventional persistent application header or row of text links must not dominate active play.

## Primary world-return control
**DECIDED:** a folded world map is the primary diegetic affordance for returning from a subject world to the Overworld.

It is a distinct object from Maittê's backpack. Phase 1A must not merge the two concepts into one permanent control.

Requirements:
- large child-friendly touch target;
- programmatic accessible label;
- visible keyboard focus for testing;
- interaction can visually unfold/lift before the spatial zoom-out;
- reduced-motion mode removes decorative movement while preserving the action;
- it must not appear as a conventional text link such as “Voltar ao mapa”.

Exact screen position and final illustration are PROVISIONAL.

## Backpack
**FUTURE / concept-only in Phase 1A:** Maittê's backpack is the secondary container for future functions such as profile/Meu Cantinho, clothing/customization, collection/achievements and settings.

Phase 1A may reserve or conceptually show the backpack, but must not build those systems merely for completeness. Parent/admin access is not automatically owned by the backpack and remains a separate product decision.

## Overworld
The Overworld itself is the home/navigation surface. Subject worlds are selected by interacting with their illustrated regions, not cards or a menu list.

## Challenge Stage
Challenge entry/exit is contextual to the Activity Slot and should not introduce a separate global navigation chrome. Exiting/finishing a challenge returns to the same Board context.

## Accessibility
Diegetic controls must remain discoverable and accessible through semantic buttons/links, programmatic labels, focus states and adequate touch targets. Visual integration must not hide functionality from assistive technologies.
