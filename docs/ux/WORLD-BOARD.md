# World Board

**Status:** DECIDED.

Entering a subject-world reveals the world itself as a large board/circuit integrated into the biome — not a conventional level-selection grid.

## Board model
- Maittê occupies a visible current space.
- A route of sequential spaces traverses landmarks such as forest, castle, ruins, water, dunes or other biome-appropriate scenery.
- Each playable space corresponds to an Activity Slot/challenge.
- The player advances space by space by completing challenges; there is no dice roll.
- Local progression may be sequential even though subject-world selection is open globally.

## Challenge transition
Selecting the current playable space enters Challenge Stage. After successful completion:
1. challenge resolves;
2. return to the board;
3. restored color visibly propagates to the completed area;
4. Maittê animates/moves to the next space;
5. next space becomes available;
6. player may continue or return to the Overworld/another world.

## Color state
The board begins substantially line-art/desaturated because color was stolen. Restoration is progressive by route/region and must be backed by state, not be a purely decorative one-off animation.

## Landmarks
Landmarks create anticipation and narrative orientation. They must not permanently encode curriculum concepts (avoid hardwiring scenery such as a permanent 'Addition Dune' if that prevents future content changes).
