# Feedback

**Status:** DECIDED interaction principles; exact assets/timing PROVISIONAL.

## Purpose

Feedback tells the child what happened and what to do next without turning mistakes into punishment.

The feedback system combines:

- character acting;
- short spoken/text response;
- object/world response;
- sound;
- restoration/progression when appropriate.

## Success

Success should communicate understanding and causality before spectacle.

Preferred sequence:

1. interaction resolves correctly;
2. puzzle/object responds immediately;
3. Maittê and/or companion reacts positively;
4. short positive sound/voice cue;
5. challenge resolves;
6. Board context returns;
7. local color restoration becomes visible;
8. Maittê moves/looks toward the restored result.

Avoid making a score popup, confetti burst or generic green button the dominant success language.

## Retry / incorrect outcome

Do not use:

- punitive red X as the primary feedback;
- harsh buzzer/alarm;
- XP loss;
- backward movement;
- sad/shaming character acting;
- language that implies the child is incapable.

Preferred sequence:

1. soft object/selection response indicates the current arrangement did not resolve;
2. companion/Maittê shifts to thoughtful/curious acting;
3. short supportive response;
4. essential instruction may be replayed;
5. authorized support/hint may be offered/escalated;
6. child retries without losing progress.

## Immediate interaction feedback

Touch interactions should visibly acknowledge input quickly:

- selected object lifts/pulses/scales subtly;
- placement target responds;
- successful placement settles/snaps;
- invalid precision/no-drop situations should not be recorded as educational errors when tap-to-place fallback exists.

## Audio feedback

Success/retry sounds follow `AUDIO.md`.

They must be short and subordinate to spoken guidance.

## Character feedback

Use the acting states in `CHARACTER-ART.md`.

Maittê and pets react to the task, not to an abstract score.

Examples:

- success: smile, look at restored object, small hop;
- retry: head tilt, thoughtful look toward manipulables;
- hint: pet points/looks toward relevant area without revealing the answer unless Discover mode explicitly allows it.

## Text feedback

Keep child-facing feedback short.

Examples of tone:

- `Isso!`
- `Boa!`
- `Vamos tentar de outro jeito.`
- `Olha mais uma vez.`

Exact authored wording belongs to content/support configuration and must remain adjustable.

## Accessibility

Feedback must not rely on one channel only.

Success/retry should have at least two of:

- visual change;
- motion/acting;
- text/caption;
- audio.

Reduced-motion mode preserves visual/text/audio meaning without requiring movement.

## Evidence boundary

Feedback renders the result received from the evaluation/orchestration layer.

It must not decide correctness, mastery or progression.

## Acceptance criteria — Phase 1B

1. Correct and retry outcomes are understandable without a red/green form convention.
2. Retry never removes progress/XP or moves Maittê backward.
3. Retry includes a path to replay instruction/support.
4. Character reactions match success/thoughtful states.
5. Feedback components do not evaluate answers.
6. Reduced-motion mode remains understandable.
