# Content Parametrization

**Status:** DECIDED conceptual model; schema details PROVISIONAL.

Educational content must be data-driven enough that changing numbers, words, prompts, difficulty or curricular packs does not require redesigning the world or rewriting a bespoke component.

## Conceptual entities
- Subject
- Curriculum
- Skill
- Learning Objective
- Content Pack
- Activity
- Puzzle Template
- Difficulty
- Support Level
- Guide/Companion
- Activity Slot

## Content Pack minimum metadata
A pack should be able to identify:
- subject and grade;
- skill/internal skill code;
- official curriculum reference when validated;
- school/source material reference;
- topic and study period where relevant;
- difficulty dimensions;
- eligible puzzle templates;
- prompts/items/answers or generation constraints;
- representation requirements;
- feedback/hint content.

## Rules
1. World/biome is not the owner of curricular content.
2. Puzzle template is not the owner of curricular content.
3. Content can be reassigned to compatible Activity Slots without rewriting scenery.
4. Random/generated variants must remain inside validated pedagogical constraints.
5. AI-generated educational items are not automatically trusted; validation rules and human/source authority prevail.
