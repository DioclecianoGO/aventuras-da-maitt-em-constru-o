# Science — Grade 2 Source Map

**Status:** DECIDED source authority and audit method; page-level reconstruction remains a living specification.

## Purpose

This document records what the supplied school material actually supports before that content is converted into game Skills, Content Packs or Activities.

It also audits the secondary review packet **Aventuras da Maittê — Expedição Natureza — Caderno de Ciências**. The packet is useful as an activity-design aid, but it is not allowed to become the sole curriculum authority.

## Source authority

Use the following precedence for Grade 2 Science content:

1. **Primary:** photographed school textbook/workbook pages supplied by the user.
2. **Primary when explicitly available:** direct teacher guidance, highlighted study priorities or assigned review scope.
3. **Secondary:** the Claude-produced review packet `Expedição Natureza`.
4. **Reference/validation:** official curriculum references such as BNCC/Paraná when separately verified and mapped.

A secondary packet may reorganize, simplify or add examples. That is acceptable only when the underlying learning objective remains source-supported and the adaptation is identified.

Do not infer that an item is school-required merely because it appears in the Claude packet.

# 2. Source-audit labels

These labels describe source fidelity and are separate from the repository governance states `DECIDED / PROVISIONAL / FUTURE / OUT_OF_SCOPE`.

- `SOURCE-CONFIRMED` — directly supported by the photographed textbook sequence.
- `ADAPTATION` — the secondary packet changes examples or exercise presentation while preserving a source-supported concept.
- `OMISSION` — a source-supported concept is materially absent or underrepresented in the secondary packet.
- `SOURCE-GAP` — the supplied primary source is missing or unreadable for the relevant claim.
- `SECONDARY-ONLY` — present in the Claude packet but not yet confirmed in the primary source.

`SECONDARY-ONLY` does not mean false. It means the game must not treat the claim as curriculum truth until primary/official validation occurs.

# 4. Audit of the Claude review packet as a whole

The packet is **approved as a secondary activity-design reference**, not as curriculum authority.

## Strong/useful aspects

- It correctly concentrates many source-supported review concepts into concrete missions.
- It frequently asks for reasoning rather than only a label.
- It intentionally tests common conceptual confusions such as appearance versus biological characteristic, habitat versus feeding, and metamorphosis versus gradual growth.
- It provides useful ideas for embodied/off-screen practice.

## Corrections to product use

1. Do not accept the packet's claim that it necessarily covers **all** studied content; observation, comparison/grouping criteria, environment composition and broad symbiosis/interdependence are underrepresented.
2. Do not convert A1–E3 into the game's canonical skill taxonomy. They are worksheet mission identifiers, not durable curricular IDs.
3. Do not copy long written prompts into the child experience; essential instructions remain spoken/replayable and image-first.
4. Do not turn packet examples into universal biological rules.
5. Do not treat the packet's suggested mastery heuristic — autonomous explanation on two different days — as engine policy. It is useful study guidance, but repository mastery thresholds remain unresolved unless separately approved.
6. Free-response explanations are valuable evidence but need an interaction/assessment design appropriate to the current no-ASR MVP; do not pretend an unvalidated text/voice evaluator can judge open scientific explanations automatically.
