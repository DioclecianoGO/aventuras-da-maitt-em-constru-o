# Art Direction

**Status:** DECIDED direction; final assets PROVISIONAL.

## Core language
2D illustrated storybook/coloring-book world: strong clean line work, warm handcrafted shapes, child-friendly detail and large readable silhouettes. Add a light manga/anime influence in expressions, movement and character appeal without turning the product into a direct imitation of any named work.

## Color as system
The world begins largely uncolored/line-art after the theft. Color restoration is a game-state visualization. Completed areas progressively fill with color while untouched areas preserve the stolen-color state.

The visual system must support partial restoration at:
- Maittê/avatar level;
- local board/landmark level;
- subject-world representation in the Overworld.

## UI integration
Avoid floating corporate/dashboard cards as the dominant visual language. Controls should feel embedded in the illustrated world. Text remains readable and accessible; diegetic UI must not sacrifice usability.

## Animation
Prioritize meaningful animation: world zoom, Maittê moving between spaces, color flowing/restoring, character reactions, companion entrances, puzzle success/failure feedback. Rich animation is desirable but must degrade gracefully on MVP constraints.

## Transition language
- Overworld → world: spatial zoom.
- Major narrative/context transition: page-turn/book metaphor when appropriate.
- Puzzle entry/exit: should preserve spatial continuity with the board rather than feel like navigating to an unrelated web form.

## Originality
References discussed during ideation are mood/direction references only. Final characters, environments and assets must be original rather than reproductions of existing copyrighted characters or exact styles.
